# Parasite SEO playbook — extramovies.org (2026-10-07)

Parasite SEO = ranking on someone else's authority while ours is zero
(new domain) or suspect (inherited pirate profile). Rent reach now, own
the asset later. It does NOT fix the domain decision (disavow-or-move
still stands) — it buys discovery, branded searches, and referral traffic
during months 1–6.

## Rules (violate none)

1. **Canonical discipline**: every syndicated review points canonical (or
   first-published claim + link) at extramovies.org. Never let a copy
   outrank the original — publish on OUR domain first, wait for indexation,
   then syndicate excerpts (not full text) outward.
2. **No spam**: platform accounts that only drop links get banned and the
   links die with them. Participation first, links as citations.
3. **Nofollow reality**: Reddit/Quora/Medium links are nofollow/UGC — they
   pass ~zero link equity. Their value is RANKING on the host + referral
   clicks + brand-query volume, not backlinks. Track them as traffic
   sources, never as link building.
4. **No reputation-abuse adjacency**: we do not publish on coupon farms,
   link schemes, or "write for us" SEO blogs (site-reputation-abuse policy
   cuts both directions).

## Channels, ranked by fit

| Channel | Play | Effort | Honest return |
|---|---|---|---|
| Letterboxd | Mirror every site review; complete profile (bio → extramovies.org link); lists ("Top 2026 horror") | Low (copy exists) | Highest fit: film-native audience, review format, dofollow-ish profile presence, referral clicks |
| Reddit (r/movies + film subs) | Genuine participation; post full reviews as discussion posts where rules allow; answer "worth watching?" threads with verdict + link | Medium | Can rank threads for long-tails; strict anti-spam — 90/10 participation rule or insta-ban |
| YouTube | Verdict-first shorts per review (30–60s) + link in description/pin | High | Biggest upside (Discovery surface, compounding subs); biggest cost |
| Medium/Substack | Import-syndicate with canonical set to our URL; "originally published at" footer link | Low | Ranks Medium domain for mid-tails; canonical protects us |
| Quora | Answer "is X worth watching / ending meaning" with real verdict + citation link | Low-Med | Direct PAA-intent match; answers can outrank our domain for a year |

## What success looks like (months 1–6)

- Referral sessions from these channels in analytics (UTM: `?utm_source=letterboxd` etc.)
- Rising exact-brand searches in Search Console (people who met us elsewhere)
- Zero dependence: if a channel bans us, the money site loses a trickle, not its foundation

## Explicitly NOT the strategy

- Buying placements, link insertions, or "parasite pages" on hacked/expired
  domains (spam, manual-action bait — especially suicidal on OUR history).
- Spinning review text across platforms (duplicate-content cannibalization;
  excerpts + canonicals only).
