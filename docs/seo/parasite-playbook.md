# Parasite SEO playbook — extramovies.org (2026-10-07)

Parasite SEO = ranking on someone else's authority while ours is zero
(new domain) or suspect (inherited pirate profile). Rent reach now, own
the asset later. It does NOT fix the domain decision (disavow-or-move
still stands) — it buys discovery, branded searches, and referral traffic
during months 1–6.

## Rules (violate none)

1. **Canonical discipline**: every syndicated review points canonical (or
   first-published claim + link) at extramovies.org. Never let a copy
   outrank the original — publish on OUR domain first, wait for indexation,
   then syndicate excerpts (not full text) outward.
2. **No spam**: platform accounts that only drop links get banned and the
   links die with them. Participation first, links as citations.
3. **Nofollow reality**: Reddit/Quora/Medium links are nofollow/UGC — they
   pass ~zero link equity. Their value is RANKING on the host + referral
   clicks + brand-query volume, not backlinks. Track them as traffic
   sources, never as link building.
4. **No reputation-abuse adjacency**: we do not publish on coupon farms,
   link schemes, or "write for us" SEO blogs (site-reputation-abuse policy
   cuts both directions).

## Nissen deltas (adopted from Jesper Nissen's public methodology)

Cadence doctrine first: post daily-ish across owned profiles; treat each
profile as its own little site (complete bios, consistent NAP/brand, cross-
links between profiles). Profiles gain authority with consistency, and that
is what makes later posts index and rank on their own.

- **Facebook Groups > Pages** for ranking; YouTube **Community posts** rank
  on their own — use both, not just videos. X articles (Premium) > tweets.
- **Own subreddit** for the brand (original posts only, never cross-post the
  Facebook/LinkedIn copy). TikTok reposts of Shorts; Threads/Bluesky/Tumblr
  as the long tail (Tumblr doubles as indexer-fodder links to the posts).
- **Profile/foundation links**: about.me, linktr.ee and equivalents, steadily
  built, interlinked, entity-consistent. Harmless, do it.
- **Press releases** (ABNewswire-style): brand story + homepage link only.
  Expect discovery/branded lift, not ranking equity; never keyword anchors.
- **AI parasites** (Perplexity/Qwen pages): only for OUR brand queries
  ("extramovies review" defense against pirate SERP pollution) — never for
  third-party/commercial terms.
- **Tiering rule (hard)**: parasites link to the money site sparingly, with
  branded/naked anchors and genuinely useful content. Daisy chains and
  tiered blasts point at parasites, never directly at extramovies.org —
  especially given our inherited profile. This is the line between his
  method working for us and it getting us penalized.

## What success looks like (months 1–6)

- Referral sessions from these channels in analytics (UTM: `?utm_source=letterboxd` etc.)
- Rising exact-brand searches in Search Console (people who met us elsewhere)
- Zero dependence: if a channel bans us, the money site loses a trickle, not its foundation

## Explicitly NOT the strategy

- Buying placements, link insertions, or "parasite pages" on hacked/expired
  domains (spam, manual-action bait — especially suicidal on OUR history).
- Spinning review text across platforms (duplicate-content cannibalization;
  excerpts + canonicals only).
- Cloud-link stacks pointed at the money domain. If ever tested: tier-2
  only, and expect decay, not compounding.

## Decay disclaimer (even Nissen has a "risk and longevity" chapter)

Parasites rent, and rent can rise: platforms deindex UGC waves, subreddits
ban, Medium paywalls, and Google periodically discounts the exact surfaces
working today (it has before — the current UGC window is a window, not a
law). His "ranked #1 in 24 hours" cases are long-tail/brand terms; do not
extrapolate them to competitive queries. Every parasite post should be
reproducible from our canonical review in under 15 minutes — never an asset
we can't afford to lose overnight.
