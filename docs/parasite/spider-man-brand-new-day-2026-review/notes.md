## Risk notes

- **Decay.** The spike decays faster than the other titles in this batch. Reddit and Medium can both be gone inside a month. Letterboxd and the YouTube channel are the durable parts.
- **Sub rules.** r/marvel removes self-promotional posts and does not tolerate accounts with empty comment histories. Link in a first comment, after two or three real replies, never in the body.
- **If a post is removed.** Do not repost the same text. Do not edit the site to chase it. Move on to the next platform in the plan.
- **Duplicate content.** The Letterboxd body, the Medium article and the Quora answer carry the same verdict and the same numbers, in different structures. Do not paste our review text into any of them.
- **Quote integrity.** The Guardian, Variety and Los Angeles Times quotes are the load-bearing part of both the Reddit post and the Medium article. Check every quote against our review before posting.
- **Footage rights.** Marvel clips in a Short are the highest takedown risk in this folder. Official trailer footage only, commentary over it, credited.
