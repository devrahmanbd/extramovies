## Channel description to paste

```
Extramovies is an independent film-review magazine: original criticism with our own rating, no links to piracy, streaming availability checked and dated.

One rating out of ten, awarded by the editor after the argument is written. Full reviews, the critic numbers and the spoiler sections are published on extramovies.org: https://extramovies.org
```

The first line is the one-line description used word for word on every other platform in
this kit. The second line is what a reader gets when they ask what the channel is for.

Keep `https://extramovies.org` in the description. Do not shorten it to `extramovies.org`
without the scheme, and do not link a single review instead of the root. The root is the
brand.

Add `https://extramovies.org` as the primary link. If a second slot is used, the second

## Profile photo and banner guidance

**Avatar (the small square, 800 by 800):** the same file used for the Letterboxd
profile, the X avatar and the Medium avatar. The wordmark **Extramovies** in the site's
own type, centred, on a flat dark background, legible at 40 pixels. One word, no
poster, no collage, no film still, no red curtain, no skull. It must not be
confusable with the pirate mirror domains or with the legacy Hindi channel.

**Banner (2048 by 1152):** flat background in the same two colors, the wordmark on the
right-hand side, and the one-line description plus `extramovies.org` in small type
underneath. YouTube crops the banner differently on every surface, so keep the left
third empty, because that is where the video duration is stamped on a phone.

**Do not use a face.** No stock model, no mascot, no film-poster montage. A review
magazine with a logo and a sentence is legible. A logo with a stranger's face is a
different entity.

## Owner notes

**What this channel is for.** Not traffic. A YouTube channel is the third independent
place where the string "Extramovies" is attached to film criticism rather than to
downloads, on a domain Google already trusts and indexes. The AI Overview for
`extramovies` is assembled from exactly this kind of third-party page. The channel
also serves the five largest review queries in the list, which is the largest
non-brand demand we have.

**Do not use the `#extramovies` hashtag.** The hashtag page belongs to the legacy Hindi
channel and it currently ranks #3 in the US for our head term. Posting into it feeds
the page that is currently mis-describing us. Use the film title and the review query
instead, and let the channel name carry the brand.

**Do not put the link in the first three seconds.** The hook is the film. The link goes
on the end screen and in the description. A Short that opens on a URL gets scrolled.

**Format.** Post Shorts (vertical, 1080 by 1920, captions burned in) for the first
five, because a Short is an afternoon and a long-form review is a day. The channel
compounds: what matters at the three-month mark is that there are thirty videos, not
that any one of them did well. Use only footage the channel is licensed to use, the
official trailer, official clips, or stills with credit.

**Do not read the aggregates as the verdict.** Each video carries our own rating. The
Rotten Tomatoes and Metacritic numbers are context, and the gap between them is
usually the interesting part of the argument.

**Verification.** YouTube requires a phone-verified channel before it can post. That
check uses the owner's own details and nothing in this kit needs to know about it.

**Risks**

- A new channel with five videos in five days reads as a launch, not a publication.
  After the first five, keep the cadence at one video per published review.
- The query volumes in the table are the film's head term, not the brand term. A video
  that wins `toy story 5 review` is worth more than a video that wins
  `extramovies`, because it brings people to the review and the review carries the
  brand. Do not swap the second for the first.
- Nothing here is expected to move the brand SERP in days. See `notes.md`.
