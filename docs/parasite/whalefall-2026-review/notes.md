## Risk notes

- **Decay.** Every surface here is short-lived because the audience is small. Letterboxd is the one that stays.
- **Sub rules.** r/WeAreTheMovieMakers is about filmmaking practice. A link in the body turns the post into promotion and gets it removed. First comment only, after real replies.
- **If a post is removed.** Do not repost the same text. Do not edit the site to chase it. Move on.
- **Duplicate content.** Letterboxd, Medium and Quora carry the same verdict and the same numbers in different structures. Do not paste our review text.
- **Date sensitivity.** The film was theatrical-only in the US on October 21, 2026 with no streaming or rental offer. Every availability line across the six files needs re-checking on the day of posting.
- **Box office and audience figures are dated.** The $8 million opening on 3,065 screens and the CinemaScore B are as of October 19, 2026.
- **The Austin Chronicle quote.** "Whalefall is a tonal mess" is the sharpest line in the set and it is also the most quotable. It comes from a real review, it is attributed, and it is used as evidence of a split rather than as our own verdict. Keep the attribution attached.
- **Spoilers.** The ending is the payoff of the whale. Every asset here stops before it.

## The one thing that needs the owner

**Post from an aged account.** For this title the account that matters most is the r/WeAreTheMovieMakers one, because a stranger with a link in a craft community gets ignored, and a maker with two weeks of real replies gets an answer. Build the reply history first, and post the link only when the thread has people in it.
