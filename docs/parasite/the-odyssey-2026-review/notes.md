## Risk notes

- **Decay.** The 3,233 percent rise is a spike on a small absolute volume. Do not build a schedule around this title. The assets are cheap, which is the point.
- **Sub rules.** r/Interstellar is a fan community built around one specific film. A post about a different Nolan film has to earn its place, and the title's thematic framing is what earns it. Keep that framing and do not retitle the post into a generic review question. If it gets no replies in 24 hours, leave it dead. One fallback only, r/movies the following week with the Metacritic user score as the hook, and one post, never both.
- **If a post is removed.** Do not repost the same text. Do not edit the site to chase it. Move on to the next platform in the plan.
- **Duplicate content.** Letterboxd, Medium and Quora carry the same verdict and the same numbers in different structures. Do not paste our review text.
- **Date sensitivity.** The availability facts in all six files are as of October 21, 2026. Universal's digital date is November 17, 2026. If you publish after that, the "still in cinemas, no streaming yet" paragraph is wrong and has to be updated, not left.
- **Accuracy on the IMAX claim.** This is the first feature shot entirely on IMAX film and the first feature-length commercial film released entirely in 70mm IMAX. Keep both halves of the claim, both are on the record, and do not compress it into "shot in IMAX".
