## Platform plan, in order

1. **Letterboxd** first. A 2019 title with 4.18 million Letterboxd ratings already has a huge review pool, so this post's job is the encore conversation, and Letterboxd is where film-native people log a re-release.
2. **YouTube Short** in the same window. The encore is a live theatrical event, and a Short is the fastest way to catch people while they are deciding whether to go.
3. **Medium** with the canonical set to our review URL, aimed at the mid-tails: "is it worth watching again", "what is the encore edition".
4. **Reddit** in r/movies, once the account has history there. r/marvel was the alternative and was rejected: it reaches people who have already decided to see the film, and the question worth answering is the cinema-trip question.
5. **Quora** last.

## Risk notes

- **Decay.** The encore window is the whole asset. Once the theatrical run ends, the "should I go" posts stop working and the copy has to become the 2019 review. Decide the date before you publish, not after.
- **Sub rules.** r/movies removes self-promotional posts. Link in a first comment after two or three real replies, never in the body.
- **If a post is removed.** Do not repost the same text. Do not edit the site to chase it. Move to the next platform in the plan.
- **Duplicate content.** Letterboxd, Medium and Quora carry the same verdict and the same numbers in different structures. Do not paste our review text.
- **Staleness.** The availability paragraph in every file is dated October 21, 2026. If you post this in November, re-check the encore run and the streaming rows before you paste.
- **Rights.** Marvel footage in the Short is the highest takedown risk in this folder. Official material only, commentary over it, credited.

## The one thing that needs the owner

**Post from an aged account.** For this title it means an account with real history in r/movies, and a YouTube channel that is not a week old. A brand-new YouTube channel posting a film review Short with a link in the description gets buried and, in the worst case, gets its monetization taken down on the first claim. Use an established channel, and if you do not have one, spend three weeks posting shorts that carry no link at all.
