## Risk notes

- **Decay.** The spike can reverse. Letterboxd and the YouTube channel are the durable parts of this package.
- **Sub rules.** r/film is heavily moderated and slow. First comment only, after real replies, and the link never in the body.
- **If a post is removed.** Do not repost the same text. Do not edit the site to chase it. Move on.
- **Duplicate content.** Letterboxd, Medium and Quora carry the same verdict and the same numbers in different structures. Do not paste our review text.
- **The second-half turn.** The studio asked critics to keep it quiet so audiences would meet it cold. Every asset in this package stops before it, on purpose. That restraint is a competitive asset as well as an honesty one. Do not spoil it in a reply, a comment or a second Short.
- **Date sensitivity.** The availability line is dated October 21, 2026 and the film was theatrical-only in the US at that point. Re-check before posting.
- **Star rating.** 3.0 stars, which is the lowest in this batch and matches our published 5.5 out of 10. Do not round it up. A Letterboxd review that disagrees with the site's own rating by a whole star looks like a different site.

## The one thing that needs the owner

**Post from an aged account.** r/film is the strictest audience in this batch for anything with a link in it, and a stranger's post on a small auteur film gets downvoted on sight. Two weeks of real replies in the sub first, and the link in a first comment only.
