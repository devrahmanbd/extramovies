## Target query

Primary: **toy story 5 review** (Semrush US, 3,600, trend down 80 percent).
Secondary, carried inside the assets: *is toy story 5 worth watching* (active row, `keywords-extramovies.csv:20`), *should I watch toy story 5* (row 21), *toy story 5 2026 review* (row 19).
No other title should share the "toy story 5 review" phrasing in the same week. The volume is high but the trend is down 80 percent, which means the window is short and the competition for that head term is already at its peak.

## Platform plan, in order

1. **Letterboxd** first. It is the cheapest surface, the audience is film-native, and the review format is native to the platform, so the copy reads as a review and not as an advert. Also the one surface where the site link sits in a durable place (the profile bio) rather than in a post that scrolls away.
2. **Medium** next, with the canonical set to our review URL. Medium ranks for mid-tails and the canonical protects the original.
3. **YouTube Short** alongside it. The channel compounds, the Short costs an afternoon, and the discovery surface is bigger than any of the text platforms in month three.
4. **Reddit** in r/Pixar, once the account has karma in that sub. Reddit needs participation first and a link in the body gets the post removed.
5. **Quora** last. It is the slowest to compound and the fastest to be penalised by a new account, and it indexes the answer for years.

## Anchor text

Use **our Toy Story 5 review** (naked, film title plus the word review) as the anchor on Reddit, Quora and the Medium footer. Use the **brand anchor** `Extramovies` in the Letterboxd profile bio and in the YouTube description line, where the brand is worth more than the phrase.

Never use the query string as the anchor, never chain a parasite to a parasite, and never point any of these at anything other than extramovies.org. No second link per post.

## Risk notes

- **Decay.** Letterboxd is the most durable of the five. Medium is the least. A Reddit thread can be gone in a day. Every asset here is reproducible from our canonical review in under fifteen minutes, which is the point of the whole exercise.
- **Sub rules.** r/Pixar tolerates review posts from accounts with history and removes thin ones. The link goes in a first comment after two or three real replies, never in the body.
- **If a post is removed.** Do not repost the same text. Do not edit the site to chase it. Move on to the next platform in the plan. A second removal on the same account is what turns a link into a pattern.
- **Duplicate content.** The Letterboxd body, the Medium article and the Quora answer all carry the same verdict and the same numbers. They are adaptations with different structures, different lengths and different openings. Do not paste our review into any of them.
- **Quote integrity.** The Guardian, Variety and RogerEbert quotes are what makes the Reddit post worth clicking. Check every quote against our review before posting.

## The one thing that needs the owner

**Post from an aged account.** Every platform here needs it, and Reddit needs it most: a fresh account with one post and one link gets removed, and the removal teaches the platform's filters about the account, not about the post. Before anything in this folder goes live, use an account with a real posting history in the target community, and if you do not have one, build it for two weeks first by commenting and replying, with no links at all.
