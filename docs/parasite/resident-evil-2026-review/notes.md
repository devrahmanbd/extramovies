## Risk notes

- **Decay.** The two best assets here are the Letterboxd review and the YouTube Short. Reddit and Quora can both be gone inside a month.
- **Sub rules.** r/ResidentEvil is small and hostile to promotional posts. The post carries no link in the body and the link goes in a first comment after real replies.
- **If a post is removed.** Do not repost the same text. Do not edit the site to chase it. Move on.
- **Duplicate content.** Letterboxd, Medium and Quora carry the same verdict and the same numbers in different structures. Do not paste our review text.
- **Date sensitivity.** This is the most date-sensitive title in the batch. On October 21, 2026 the film was still in US theaters with no streaming or rental offer and only a Blu-ray to buy. Every file that states this needs re-checking before posting. A wrong "where to watch" is worse than no "where to watch".
- **Audience split.** Letterboxd at 3.75 and a 96 percent Rotten Tomatoes score is the interesting part of this title. Both figures are dated October 21, 2026 and will have moved.
- **Rights.** Game footage, movie footage and score are three separate rights. Official trailer material only, commentary over it, credited.
