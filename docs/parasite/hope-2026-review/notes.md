## Risk notes

- **Decay.** Low volume, short life. Letterboxd is the durable surface.
- **Sub rules.** r/HorrorMovies is a small community with a memory for who posts in it. Link in a first comment, after real replies, never in the body.
- **Fallback, used once.** If the r/HorrorMovies post gets no replies after 72 hours, r/movies is the fallback: one post, same rule, link in the first comment. Never both.
- **No Indian sub for this title, on purpose.** Hope is a Korean film. It had an Indian theatrical run, but the Indian-language communities are there for Indian cinema, and a Korean monster film post there reads as targeting and gets the account flagged. The account is worth more than the thread.
- **If a post is removed.** Do not repost the same text. Do not edit the site to chase it. Move on.
- **Duplicate content.** Letterboxd, Medium and Quora carry the same verdict and the same numbers in different structures. Do not paste our review text.
- **Title collision, everywhere.** Every asset names Na Hong-jin and Hope Harbor in the first lines. Keep it that way. The query "hope 2026 movie review" is ambiguous on the face of it, and a reader who lands on the wrong film is worse than no reader at all.
- **Date sensitivity.** The availability paragraph is dated October 21, 2026, with the digital release on October 13, 2026. Re-check before posting.

## The one thing that needs the owner

**Post from an aged account.** For this title the important one is the Letterboxd profile, because a brand-new profile posting nine reviews in one sitting across nine films reads as a spam profile and gets the account flagged. Post these over two or three weeks, with the other films in between, and let the profile look like a person.
