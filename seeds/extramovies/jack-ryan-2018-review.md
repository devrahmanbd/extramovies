Tom Clancy's Jack Ryan is the Clancy hero rebuilt for streaming: the analyst who goes to the field, played by John Krasinski with more physical commitment than the role requires. Across four seasons on Prime Video the show followed a rare curve, getting better as it went, from 75% on Rotten Tomatoes for season 1 to 87% for the 2023 finale run. It ended where the character always did, promoted out of the action, and the franchise continued in 2026 with the feature Jack Ryan: Ghost War. As a full watch, it is the most consistently entertaining of the recent CIA series.

> **Spoiler-free verdict:** Yes, worth watching, and the middle seasons are the reward. Big-budget espionage with real location work, a committed lead, and a finale that closes the loop. Start at season 1 and let it build.

## What Jack Ryan is about

Created by Graham Roland and Carlton Cuse, the series opens on Matthew Quirk-style procedural ground: Ryan is a CIA terrorism analyst in Washington who spots suspicious bank transfers and gets pulled into the field by station chief James Greer (Wendell Pierce). Each season relocates the threat: a jihadist financier in season 1, a Venezuelan arms pipeline in season 2, a Russian deep-state conspiracy in season 3, and a final tour in season 4 that promotes Ryan inside the agency while handing the field work to Michael Peña's Ding Chavez.

The show ran from August 2018 to its July 2023 conclusion, four seasons on Prime Video. The planned Chavez spin-off never materialized, but the character survived: Peña's casting carried into the 2026 feature Jack Ryan: Ghost War, released May 20, 2026, with Krasinski and Michael Kelly returning.

## Cast and performances

Krasinski plays the intelligence first, physicality second, which suits a hero whose defining trait is pattern recognition. Wendell Pierce's Greer is the show's best character, a case officer who runs people like chess problems, and Michael Kelly's entry from season 3 gives the back half its gravitas. NPR's Eric Deggans diagnosed the dynamic precisely in his season 1 review: the show "is most successful when it shifts attention away from its star." The ensemble around the desk is what makes the field work pay off.

## What works

Scale without confusion. The seasons shoot in Morocco, Moscow, London, Colombia and Prague with staging that lets the geography read, and the action beats, particularly the season 3 and 4 finales, are engineered with a clarity most streamer thrillers skip. The show also understands the Clancy machinery: analyst work, dead drops, and interagency knives get as much screen time as the shootouts. RT critics credited exactly that in the late seasons, with season 3 praised for tighter pacing and season 4 landing the show's best score.

## What doesn't

Season 2 is the wobble. The Venezuela storyline asks its politics to do more work than its writing supports, and the RT critics score dipped to 67% for that run. Krasinski's Ryan also stays a touch too clean for a man four years into the job, and the romantic subplots are the first thing to skip on a rewatch.

## What audiences say

RT audience reviews skew generous, with viewers grading the four-season run as a five-star franchise on acting and plotting, and the season 3 bounce validated the habit of watching week to week. The final seasons landed hardest with the people who had stayed: season 4 arrived at 87% from critics, the show's high, which almost never happens to a spy series on its way out. TMDB has it at 7.7 from 1,918 votes, checked October 24, 2026.

## Verdict

Is Jack Ryan worth watching? Yes, all four seasons, with patience for the second. It is a franchise show in the best sense: the budget goes on screen, the seasons build on each other, and the finale, in July 2023, closed Ryan's field career without insulting the audience. The 2026 Ghost War film extends the story for people who finish the series hungry. As an entry point to modern TV espionage, this is the one that respects your time.
