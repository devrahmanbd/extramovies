## The plot the records carry

TMDB's synopsis runs this way: nearly two decades after seizing control of the Imperium, Paul Atreides rules as a ruthless Emperor and must face the consequences of his reign as old allies return, new threats gather and betrayal waits in every shadow. He is haunted by visions of imperial collapse and by the return of his long-lost love, with Chani at the heart of the conspiracy that closes in. That synopsis tracks the film's source directly: Frank Herbert's Dune Messiah, first published in 1969, a book about the cost of Paul's victory rather than the victory itself, per Wikipedia's film page. The same page verifies the credits behind it: a screenplay by Villeneuve with Brian K. Vaughan, cinematography by Linus Sandgren, editing by Joe Walker, music by Hans Zimmer, and filming that wrapped on November 11, 2025.

The first two films split Herbert's 1965 novel Dune across two pictures. Part Three is the first Villeneuve Dune that adapts a new text whole.

## The cast and their characters

Timothée Chalamet returns as Paul Atreides, an Emperor by the synopsis's own word; Zendaya returns as Chani. Jason Momoa rejoins the story as Hayt, the figure Wikipedia's page describes as the resurrected Duncan Idaho. Florence Pugh plays Princess Irulan. Rebecca Ferguson is back as Lady Jessica. Robert Pattinson joins the series as Scytale. Anya Taylor-Joy plays Alia Atreides. Javier Bardem is Stilgar, Charlotte Rampling is Reverend Mother Mohiam, and Isaach de Bankolé plays Farok. Josh Brolin reprises Gurney Halleck, per Wikipedia. Every other name above is a credit TMDB's cast list carries.

## Why the first two Dune films matter

The first film, released in US theaters on October 22, 2021, turned Herbert's famously unfilmable novel into a $410,668,500 worldwide gross on TMDB's records, an 83 percent Tomatometer from 512 critics, and 90 percent from more than 5,000 audience ratings on the Popcornmeter, both checked November 10, 2026. Part Two, in US theaters March 1, 2024, grossed $714,844,358 worldwide and reached a 92 percent Tomatometer from 466 critics with a 95 percent Popcornmeter. TMDB carries 7.8 for the first film from 15,917 votes and 8.1 for Part Two from 8,933 votes. Both carry a PG-13 in the US, and both returned to American theaters on November 13, 2026, as rerelease entries on TMDB. Combined, the two serve a worldwide gross of about $1.13 billion, and the questions their endings left open are precisely what the "epic conclusion" tagline answers.
