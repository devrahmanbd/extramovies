# Avengers: Doomsday (2026): Release Date, Cast, and What Is Confirmed

Avengers: Doomsday puts Robert Downey Jr. back in the Marvel Cinematic Universe as Victor von Doom, and it opens in theaters in December 2026. TMDB carries the film in post production with a 165-minute runtime, Anthony and Joe Russo directing, and Marvel Studios, Kevin Feige Productions and AGBO producing. The official trailer went up on July 20, 2026, the same day ticket sales opened, and it has since passed 83.7 million views on the Marvel Entertainment channel, checked November 10, 2026. This preview keeps to what is confirmed: the dates, the cast TMDB carries, the trailer record, and the endgame history that gives the film its meaning.

## Avengers: Doomsday release date

The rollout is week-wide, and the date depends on the market. TMDB books the wide release for most territories, the United Kingdom and Germany included, on December 16, 2026. India opens on December 17, 2026. The United States and Canada sit on December 18, 2026, the date Marvel's own announcement carries. Ticket sales for the film opened on July 20, 2026, per Marvel's announcement of the trailer and its Infinity Vision certification, and TMDB also logs a Los Angeles premiere entry for December 14, 2026. No streaming date is on TMDB's provider list, and none has been announced; the film opens theatrical first, and any streaming claim before December would be invented.

## What Avengers: Doomsday is about

Marvel's logline holds the premise in one sentence: beloved heroes from three distinct universes are set on a deadly collision course and face an existential threat unlike anything they have ever encountered. The three-universe framing explains a cast list that reads like a lock-down of two decades of Marvel screen history, and Deadline reported that the film was announced at Comic-Con 2024 along with the companion Avengers: Secret Wars, the two projects that marked the Russos' return to directing Marvel. The same Deadline report books the four Avengers films released to date at more than $7.7 billion worldwide, the commercial floor this crossover stands on. TMDB credits Stephen McFeely and Michael Waldron as the writers, with the character lineage credited to Stan Lee and Jack Kirby on the record even while the plot stays closed.

## The cast TMDB carries

Robert Downey Jr. leads the billing as Victor von Doom, the casting decision Marvel built the marketing around, and Deadline framed his arrival as "one of the most formidable villains in the MCU." Chris Evans returns as Steve Rogers, and Deadline flags it as the character's first screen appearance since Avengers: Endgame in 2019. Chris Hemsworth's Thor returns beside him. The billing also reassembles the 2000s X-Men lineup: Ian McKellen as Magneto, Patrick Stewart as Professor X, James Marsden as Cyclops, Kelsey Grammer as Beast, Rebecca Romijn as Mystique, and Alan Cumming as Nightcrawler, roles the same actors carried in the older X-Men films.

The Fantastic Four sits whole on the card: Pedro Pascal as Reed Richards, Vanessa Kirby as Sue Storm, Joseph Quinn as Johnny Storm, and Ebon Moss-Bachrach as Ben Grimm. The Avengers line runs Anthony Mackie's Sam Wilson, Sebastian Stan's Bucky Barnes, Paul Rudd's Scott Lang, Florence Pugh's Yelena Belova, Wyatt Russell's John Walker, Simu Liu's Shang-Chi, Letitia Wright's Shuri, Danny Ramirez's Joaquin Torres, Lewis Pullman's Robert Reynolds, and David Harbour's Alexei Shostakov. Tom Hiddleston's Loki, Hayley Atwell's Peggy Carter, Channing Tatum's Gambit, Kathryn Newton's Cassie Lang, Winston Duke and Tenoch Huerta Mejia fill out a card TMDB carries at 36 names.

## What the trailer confirms

The July 20 trailer does the crossover arithmetic in public footage. Deadline's report on it has Hemsworth's Thor telling the room "we're going to need a miracle," Namor and the Black Panthers in the mix, and Doctor Doom on screen in full armor. The Times of India's report on the CinemaCon footage shown to exhibitors in April 2026 describes Downey Jr. adopting a distinct accent for Victor von Doom, a Doom sequence at the X-Mansion, and a parted-ocean landscape where the Fantastic Four meet the Wakandans, notes that match the trailer's beats. The official Marvel Entertainment upload sits at 83.7 million views and 2.6 million likes as of the November 10 check, and CinemaCon footage of the same film has still never been posted online.
