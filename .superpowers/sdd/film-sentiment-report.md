## 2. TMDB user-review themes (top reviews per film)

### Dune: Part Two (16 TMDB reviews sampled, top 5 read)
Recurring praise:
- Audiovisual spectacle and scale demand big screen; sandworm sequences and battle staging singled out.
- Sound design and Zimmer score as physical, immersive experience.
- Acting ensemble elevated, with Bardem scene-stealing and Chalamet career-step claims.
- Thematic depth on prophecy, faith, power and politics beyond action.
Recurring complaints:
- Thin plot stretched over 2h46; sagging middle act noted even by high scorers.
- Under-served Chani arc relative to setup.
- Logistics nits (worm-riding mechanics, pacing of intrigue vs. battle).

### The Batman (28 TMDB reviews sampled, top 5 read)
Recurring praise:
- Noir-detective reinvention over action-comedy formula; Zodiac-like Riddler angle.
- Cinematography of Gotham and Giacchino score as best-in-class for the character.
- Pattinson vindication narrative; Dano, Kravitz, Farrell standout mentions.
- Grounded corruption plot fits this Batman.
Recurring complaints:
- ~3-hour runtime feels indulgent; second-act drag and overstuffed third act.
- Mystery too predictable; twists fail to surprise.
- Whisper-grunt vocal delivery and repetitive dialogue tics grate.

### Joker (19 TMDB reviews sampled, top 5 read)
Recurring praise:
- Phoenix performance as transformative, film-carrying; physicality and volatility.
- Grounded character study avoiding origin-formula clichés; social-neglect and mental-health framing resonates.
- Period Gotham craft, cinematography and score.
- Lean 2h02 runtime vs. bloated comic films.
Recurring complaints:
- Thin supporting cast written only to serve Arthur's arc.
- Mental-illness depiction vague and stigmatizing (illness equals danger).
- Derivative of 70s Scorsese (Taxi Driver / King of Comedy) without new insight.

## 3. Critic consensus — supporters vs. detractors (paraphrased)

### Dune: Part Two
Supporters praise:
- Generational sci-fi spectacle; craft mastery across image, sound and world-building.
- Emotional and thematic step-up from Part One; prophecy critique gives blockbuster weight.
Detractors attack:
- Vibes over story; stunning images carrying a simple rebel-attack plot stretched long.
- Cold performances and sidelined romance; momentum and closure sacrificed for franchise setup.

### The Batman
Supporters praise:
- Most serious detective-noir Batman; direction gives the character interiority and redemption arc.
- Look and sound create genuinely frightening, realistic Gotham.
Detractors attack:
- Bloated murder-mystery that does not earn three hours; meandering plot and inflated finale riddle.
- Self-serious gloom without fun; derivative of Nolan without iconic highs.

### Joker
Supporters praise:
- Scorching star vehicle; visceral, daring reinvention proving comic films need no cape battles.
- Unsettling mirror of inequality and service collapse; debate-worthy for years.
Detractors attack:
- Hollow pastiche of better films; grim affectation masking thin social commentary.
- Irresponsible messaging on violence and illness; self-important while dramatically empty.

## 4. Cross-examination

| Film | Even fans admit (weakness) | Even detractors concede (strength) |
|---|---|---|
| Dune: Part Two | Overlong with thin middle; story could run shorter. | Unmatched big-screen craft; score and imagery overwhelm. |
| The Batman | Too long and predictable; third act sags. | Gorgeous, atmospheric Gotham; strong lead and villain turns. |
| Joker | Derivative and one-note in script; supporting cast flat. | Phoenix magnetic; craft and commitment undeniable. |

Note: Joker shows the widest critic/audience split (68% vs 89% RT; 59 vs 8.9 MC); Dune 2 the tightest alignment; The Batman mid-aligned (85% vs 87%).
