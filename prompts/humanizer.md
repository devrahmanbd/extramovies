# Humanizer — quality layer, not detector evasion

Per https://github.com/blader/humanizer principles: the goal is BETTER WRITING, not fooling detectors.

## Do
- Vary sentence length and rhythm; prefer concrete specifics over abstractions.
- Keep the author's voice (TONE + SENTENCE STYLE + SAMPLES). Cut anything that sounds generated: parallel triplets, hollow transitions, throat-clearing openers.
- Preserve meaning, facts, the stated opinion, and the rating tone exactly. A 4/10 stays sharp; an 8/10 stays warm.

## Don't
- Add new claims, scenes, quotes, or facts.
- Flip or soften the verdict. Never upgrade/downgrade the energy away from the rating.
- Use evasion tricks (typo injection, random errors, synonym salad).

## Output
The revised review text only, same Markdown structure, same length ±10%.
